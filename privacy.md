# Skipless Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 October 2026

Skipless is a Safari extension, with a small companion app for macOS, that
removes ads from YouTube videos so they start right away. This policy explains
what Skipless does with your data.

## In short

Skipless does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no
accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

## What Skipless does on your device

- **Runs only on YouTube.** The extension works on pages from youtube.com and
  youtube-nocookie.com. It does not run on any other website.
- **Changes YouTube pages inside Safari.** To remove ads, Skipless reads the
  video data YouTube sends to the page and removes the ad schedule from it. If
  an ad still starts, Skipless mutes it and skips it. All of this happens in
  Safari on your device; nothing Skipless reads is sent anywhere.
- **Asks YouTube to load a video again when needed.** When YouTube holds a
  video back for an ad, Skipless asks YouTube for the same video again, with a
  few of YouTube's own playback settings changed. This request goes only to
  YouTube, as part of normal playback, and adds no information about you.
- **Keeps one setting.** Skipless stores whether ad blocking is turned on or
  off, in Safari's extension storage on your device. It is not synced or sent
  anywhere.
- **The companion app** only checks whether the extension is turned on in
  Safari, and opens Safari Settings or YouTube when you click its buttons.

## What Skipless does not do

- It does not collect or keep your browsing history, viewing history, searches
  or any other personal information.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising services.
- It does not track you across websites or apps.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone, because it has none.

## Safari's permission notice

Safari says that Skipless "can read and alter webpages" on youtube.com. Safari
uses this wording for every extension that changes a page. Skipless needs it to
remove ads from YouTube pages, as described above.

## YouTube and Google

When you watch YouTube, Google collects data under its own privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>. Skipless has no control over what
YouTube or Google collect.

## Children

Skipless is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone,
including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new
